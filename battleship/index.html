    Enemy Fleet

      Fire on Enemy Waters to hunt the rival fleet. Fleet: Carrier(5), Battleship(4), Cruiser(3), Submarine(3), Destroyer(2). A hit earns another shot — sink every ship before the enemy sinks yours.

      Vanilla HTML · CSS · JavaScript — university front-end project

      🗺️

      Captain's Orders

      • Goal: sink the entire enemy fleet before yours goes under.
      • Setup: both fleets are deployed automatically — 5 ships each, placed randomly, never overlapping.
      • Your turn: click any cell on Enemy Waters to fire a cannon shot.
      • Hit: a red blast marks a hit and you fire again immediately.
      • Miss: a splash marks the water and the turn passes to the enemy.
      • Sunk ship: once every cell of a ship is hit, it's destroyed and crossed off the fleet list.
      • Victory / Defeat: the battle ends the moment one fleet is fully sunk.
      • Use the SFX and MUSIC buttons to control sound; NEW VOYAGE restarts the battle any time.
